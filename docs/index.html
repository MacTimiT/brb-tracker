A note on the numbers.

OCR finds “Be Right Back” on sampled video frames. Durations are estimates; very short screens and OCR failures can be missed. Reason suggestions come from the preceding transcript and remain unverified until reviewed. A BRB screen measures screen time, not proof of physical absence.
